Sustainability Icons

Neon & BW

The Task

2 sustainable icon sets for 2 target groups: a version based on neon tubes for the young and digital generation and a version reduced to elegant black and white that goes with everything, primarily for middle-aged people. A natural color palette was chosen for both sets.
